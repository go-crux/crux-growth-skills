# GLOSSARY — crux-plugin

The Crux plugin: skills a client installs in their own Claude that carry Crux craft over their own Growth Brain. The brain ships mechanism, the plugin ships craft (mcp_brain ADR-0026).

> How to use: read this before planning or building in this project. Add or sharpen terms as you go (usually during a `/grill-with-docs` session). Definitions stay tight, one or two sentences, opinionated. No dates, PRD numbers or rollout status; point to the ADR instead. Budget: 80 words an entry, 400 lines this file. See `GLOSSARY-FORMAT.md` in the skill for the rules.

## Black Friday

Every window is anchored to that year's Black Friday, the last Friday of November, so years compare like for like.

- **Black Friday weekend**: Black Friday to Cyber Monday inclusive, four days. _Avoid_: BFCM, Cyber week, BF week.

- **Lead-in**: 1 November to the day before Black Friday, the run-up where early offers and teasers land. _Avoid_: pre-sale, early access.

- **Festive**: the day after Cyber Monday to 24 December. _Avoid_: Christmas period, peak.

- **Baseline**: 1 September to 31 October of the same year, the normal trading every Black Friday window is read against. Two months, so an early October teaser does not set the norm on its own. _Avoid_: BAU period, control.

- **Lift**: a window's figure over the same year's Baseline, on Shopify-basis figures only (revenue on the client's budget rung, orders, new orders, spend). The year-on-year unit every Black Friday comparison uses. _Avoid_: uplift, growth.

- **Target test**: this year's planned November and December, from the client's budget sheet (`daily_budget_versus_actuals`), turned into a Lift and judged against the last two years' actual Lift as Achievable, Stretch or Unrealistic. _Avoid_: feasibility check, sense check.

- **Pillar gap**: what new customers, order frequency and order value must each do to close a Stretch or Unrealistic target, read against last year's. _Avoid_: shortfall, variance.

- **Scenario**: one way of closing the Pillar gap through a single pillar, the others held at last year's level. _Avoid_: option, play.

- **Daily phasing**: the agreed monthly targets re-spread by last year's daily shape, aligned by days from Black Friday rather than by calendar date, so the days still add back to the month. _Avoid_: daily budget (the sheet's own weekday spread).

- **Black Friday plan**: the skill's output, one artifact with two tabs: **Last year** (the read and the Target test) and **Plan** (targets, chosen Scenario, Daily phasing, actions, opportunities, risks and the watch list), agreed with the founder in a grill. _Avoid_: report, playbook.
