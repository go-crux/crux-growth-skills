# No plan on the sheet

Open this when `<client>-daily_budget_versus_actuals` is absent from the roster, has no
`all`/`all` row for a month, or holds no plan for the revenue, orders, new orders or spend of
that month.

1. **Ask first.** Ask the founder for that month's targets: revenue on the orders table's main sales
   measure (name it), orders, new orders and spend, for each month with no plan. One question, one
   recommendation: the recommendation is the last-year level per target (step 2), shown as
   figures, so the founder can answer "yes".
2. **Test what they give.** A target the founder gives goes through the Target test as it
   stands, labelled "Founder's target" in the Source column.
3. **Propose second.** For a month or measure the founder leaves unanswered, or when they have
   no target, use the last-year level as the target and label it "Proposed: last year's Lift on
   this year's Baseline". It reads Achievable by construction, so no Pillar gap follows for it.
4. A measure the sheet does plan keeps its sheet figure, labelled "Budget sheet".
