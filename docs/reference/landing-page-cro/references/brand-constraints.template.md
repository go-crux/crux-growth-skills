# Brand constraints — template

Copy this file to `brand-constraints.<brand>.md` and fill it in. Everything
client-specific in this skill lives here and nowhere else; the framework files are
brand-neutral by design.

If no constraints file exists for the brand in scope, run the interview at the
bottom first. It takes one exchange, and the answers are worth saving.

Delete sections that genuinely do not apply. Do not leave a section as a
placeholder — an unfilled heading reads as "no constraint" and will be treated as
one.

## Banned conversion levers

Generic CRO recommends these; this brand does not use them. List each with the
reason, because the reason is what lets a critique propose the right substitute.

- `<lever>` — `<why>`

discounting as the primary offer lever, exit-intent popups, fake or inflated

## Pricing and offer policy

- Public prices, and where each is shown.
- Whether prices may differ by surface, and why.
- Discounting policy: permitted, and in what form.
- Subscription and plan structure; what may be defaulted.
- Bundles: where they may appear.
- Any price testing rules — for instance, tests only on unindexed pages to
  prevent price discovery.

---

## Proof and social-proof policy

- Testimonial policy: permitted, and under what approval.
- Which proof types are available now, and which are gated pending assets,
  approval or legal sign-off.
- Named ambassadors, experts or advisers, and how each may be framed.
- Anything explicitly forbidden — inventing testimonials, using unverified
  reviews, implying endorsement.

---

## Competitor policy

- May competitors be named? If not, what is the approved contrast device?
- Category-level contrast phrasing that is permitted.

---

## Copy register

- Language variant and spelling.
- Headline conventions: case, punctuation.
- Sentence and adjective rules.
- Punctuation the brand does not use.
- Preferred and forbidden word choices, with the substitutions.
- Trademark usage: which marks, on which mention.

---

## Design constraints

- Colour rules, including any semantic colour-to-meaning mapping that limits how
  colours may be combined.
- Typography, corner radii, imagery, texture conventions.
- Aesthetic drift to avoid — the specific look the brand is often mistaken for
  and must not become.
- Which CTA styles are approved.

# The interview

When no constraints file exists, ask these in one batch. Six answers are enough to
run a safe critique; offer to save them as a new constraints file afterwards.

1. **Levers off the table.** Is there anything a conversion consultant would
   normally recommend that this brand will not do — urgency, discounting,
   competitor comparison, popups?
2. **Claims.** What can you say about efficacy, and what are you forbidden from
   saying? Any regulated language? Any approved statistics, with sources?
3. **Price and offer policy.** What is the public price, is discounting allowed,
   and is there a subscription or plan structure with rules about defaults?
4. **Proof.** Can you use testimonials today? What proof assets exist — reviews,
   awards, lab results, experts, press — and which are gated?
5. **Register.** Any house style rules I should keep to — spelling, headline
   conventions, words you avoid, trademark usage?
6. **Segments.** Who are the distinct audiences for this page, and what does each
   one most need to hear?

If the answers to any of these are "no constraint", record that explicitly rather
than leaving the section blank.
