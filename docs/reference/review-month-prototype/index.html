Acquisition

New customers slipped 4.5% to 2,100 as paid social grew dearer; search held its cost.

  1. Blended CAC rose 10% to £40 as visits held and new-customer conversion fell 4.0%
  2. Paid social’s CAC rose 22% to £52, the steepest of the top five channels, as its new orders fell 15% to 450
  3. Paid channels brought 61% of new orders, a share 3.0% below August’s
  4. Meta: three prospecting campaigns spent £9k above a £70 CAC
  5. Google: brand search kept a £14 CAC on 310 new orders
  6. Customers from email are worth £180 in their first year, the most of any channel
  7. Three actions are worth about £38k a month, led by moving paid social’s weakest spend into search

Definitions

New customer: a first order ever in the month.

CAC: ad spend over new customers, blended.

MER: revenue over marketing spend.

CM2: revenue less product, delivery, payment and marketing costs.

Caveats

Revenue is ex VAT and net of refunds, dated by order.

Every % change is relative: a rate moving from 2.0% to 1.8% reads −10%.

New orders with no known channel are shown as their own row, never spread across channels.
