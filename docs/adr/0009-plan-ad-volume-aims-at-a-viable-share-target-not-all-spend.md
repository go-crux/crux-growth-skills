# plan-ad-volume aims at a Viable share target, not all spend

**Status:** Accepted, 2026-11-16. Extends [ADR-0008](0008-plan-ad-volume-counts-every-launched-ad-and-reads-a-hit-rate.md).

ADR-0006 and ADR-0008 sized the Viable ad stock as daily spend over Spend per viable ad, which assumes every pound of spend sits on a viable ad. No account achieves that: some spend always goes to ads in their test phase and to ads that have stopped working, and in a test on three real accounts even their best weeks put well under half of spend on viable ads, so the formula overstated the viable ads needed several times over, and with it the gap, the Catch-up, the launches a week and the scenarios. The Viable ad stock is now daily spend times a Viable share target over Spend per viable ad, the target being the smaller of 100% and the 90th-percentile weekly Viable share times 1.2: the best share of spend the account has sustained, plus 20% for running creative production well. The weeks read leave out the window's first 6 complete weeks, which lean towards Ramping while the weekday index, life spend and Peak CAC warm up, and any week below 25% of the median weekly spend, which gives a noisy share; the run stops where none is left. We accept a target that rests on the account's own history rather than a published benchmark, and so differs account to account, because a benchmark would be a number we cannot ground and the account's own best weeks show what its creative production has already reached.
