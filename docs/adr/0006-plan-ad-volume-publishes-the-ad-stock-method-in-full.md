# plan-ad-volume publishes the ad stock method in full, and computes it on every Source

**Status:** Accepted, 2026-11-09. Extends [ADR-0005](0005-audit-ads-publishes-the-fatigue-method-in-full.md).

`plan-ad-volume` sizes how many new Meta ads a brand needs each week with the ad stock model we run in client builds: a survival curve of the share of ads still Healthy at each day of age (an age counted only with 3 or more ads), the area under it as the Viable lifespan, the survival-weighted daily spend per viable ad, and Little's law read backwards, Viable ad stock (daily spend over spend per viable ad) divided by the Viable lifespan, times 7. We publish it in full in this public repo for the reason ADR-0005 gives: the plugin ships craft and the brain ships mechanism, and keeping the method in the brain would make the skill brain-only, which ADR-0004 rules out. The skill computes the curve itself from ad-by-day rows on every Source, a Growth Brain included, rather than reading the client builds' survival curve and forecast marts, which differ client to client and are not client-facing; so one method gives one answer whatever the Source, and a brain changes the attribution and the history, never the maths. Viable means the audit-ads Healthy status (Winner included), so the skill carries its own copy of audit-ads' classification, and the two copies must change together. Once published the method cannot be taken back, and a client build that tunes it later diverges from the public skill unless both change.
