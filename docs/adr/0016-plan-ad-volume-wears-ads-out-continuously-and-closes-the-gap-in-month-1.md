# plan-ad-volume wears ads out continuously and closes the gap in month 1

**Status:** Accepted 2026-11-19. Amends [ADR-0008](0008-plan-ad-volume-counts-every-launched-ad-and-reads-a-hit-rate.md) (the Wearing out and Catch-up rules) and [ADR-0014](0014-plan-ad-volume-is-a-monthly-creative-deep-dive.md) (the month-by-month plan).

The month step took a month's Wearing out as the stock times the month's days over Days viable per hit, capped at the whole stock, and aimed each month at a target that glided from today's stock to the stock needed by the Budget month. Where Days viable per hit was under a month's days (about a month on real accounts), the cap bit and the step ignored that ads launched during the month also wear out within it, so Ads to launch fell below `ads_for_spend` even with the stock at target: a dry run read a headline of Ads needed each month above the breakdown's Total, with the stock check saying the account had more working ads than its spend needs, and a reader could not see why to launch more. With turnover about once a month the glide only made month 1 read lower than steady state while the account was short, which reads backwards, and the breakdown, built from the stock gap, showed being slightly ahead as a large "To grow" minus with nothing saying why having enough today does not cut the monthly number.

Working ads now stop at a steady rate of 1 over Days viable per hit a day, the ones launched during the month included. For a month of d days, q = e^(−d ÷ D) is the share of the starting stock still working at its end and a = D × (1 − q) ÷ d the average share of a month's launches still working at its end; Ads to launch is the larger of 0 and (target − stock × q) over (Hit rate × a), rounded up, the end stock is stock × q plus Expected to work × a, and the Roadmap's Wearing out is every working ad that stops in the month. At the stock equal to the target the figure is `ads_for_spend` of the month's spend exactly, whatever D is. The target is the stock needed in every month, so the Catch-up closes the gap in month 1, once; the glide, the total catch-up launches and the special case for a short Days viable per hit are removed. Next month's ads are shown from the headline: To stay level (the Steady-state number up to the month's length), To catch up or Ahead (one-off, negative where today's stock is above what spend needs) and To grow (the change in stock needed), which add to month 1's Ads to launch, with the stock check noting that having enough today does not lower the monthly number. We accept a bigger month 1 while an account is short, and a month 1 below steady state while it is ahead, because each now reads as what it is: the one-off adjustment to a number that is otherwise about the Steady-state number every month.
