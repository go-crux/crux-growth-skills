# plan-ad-volume is a monthly creative deep dive

**Status:** Amended by [ADR-0016](0016-plan-ad-volume-wears-ads-out-continuously-and-closes-the-gap-in-month-1.md), 2026-11-19, which replaces the month-by-month plan's step; the binomial chance of finding no outlier was dropped in QA (#286), so Expected outliers a month stands alone in the Outlier engine. Accepted 2026-11-18. Supersedes the page half of [ADR-0011](0011-plan-ad-volume-is-a-scorecard-read-against-the-accounts-own-best.md); its Lever best and Limiting factor stand.

The page is rebuilt on the layout of CTC's Creative Deep Dive, the report a founder already recognises: the Creative score, Spending power, the Outlier engine, Creative rotation, Ads that work, and Work backwards from spend, where a slider runs the Steady-state number as New ads = spend × Viable share target ÷ (Hit rate × Viable spend per hit), the same identity as before written so it can be checked on a calculator, with Expected outliers a month and the binomial chance of finding none. The plan moves from weeks to calendar months, because the question is how many ads to brief for next month and outliers are too rare for a weekly chance to read anything but zero; the measurements stay weekly where they need the sample. Next run is monthly, around the 20th. The page is always Crux-branded on the editorial system of ADR-0003, so plan-ad-volume carries its own copy of `crux-brand.md` and loads its fonts from Google Fonts with a system fallback, and it gains one inline script for the slider, with no network call, while still showing the answer at current spend and at the Budget without JavaScript. That ends the no-script, no-network page of ADR-0011. The Budget ladder goes, since the slider covers any spend, and so does the three-panel Scorecard; the Limiting factor stays, read under Work backwards from spend. The Format split moves from launch counts to spend share, a reversible change with no ADR of its own.
