# plan-ad-volume counts a hit only if its first-28-day CAC is within 1.2× the account's

**Status:** Accepted 2026-11-19. Amends [ADR-0013](0013-plan-ad-volume-counts-a-hit-only-once-it-clears-a-spend-bar.md) and the Hit rate and Viable after week 1 anchors of [ADR-0012](0012-plan-ad-volume-scores-creative-health-on-fixed-published-bands.md).

Under ADR-0013 a hit was viable on at least one day in its first 28 and spent the Spend bar over them, so an ad that started well, with one 5-day stretch at a good CAC, and was poor for the rest of its first month still counted. A hit must now also hold a good CAC over the whole of its first 28 days: its spend over its new orders in those days is no more than 1.2 times the account's over the same 28 calendar days (every ad outside the awareness and lead campaigns), and an ad with no new orders fails. 1.2 is the Low efficiency line `audit-ads` already uses, so one number says "dear" in both skills. The test changes how the Viable lifespan splits between the Hit rate and the Days viable per hit, not the Viable lifespan itself, so the ad count does not move: the Hit rate falls, the Days viable per hit rises, and the Hit rate and Viable after week 1 measures of the Creative score read what they say. The ads that spent the bar but are not hits (never viable, or over the CAC line) are one count of the base, with hits and low-spend ads, and the Low-spend rate is unchanged.

The stricter test lowers both measures on every account, so the fixed anchors of ADR-0012 are rescaled from our own accounts, rounded, and stay provisional like the rest: Hit rate Poor 2%, Average 5%, Good 10% (was 3, 8 and 15), and Viable after week 1 Poor 15%, Average 35%, Good 60% (was 10, 25 and 45).
