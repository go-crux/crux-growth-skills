# plan-ad-volume names a Focus from the hit rate, and does not cap the plan

**Status:** Superseded by [ADR-0011](0011-plan-ad-volume-is-a-scorecard-read-against-the-accounts-own-best.md), 2026-11-17. Accepted 2026-11-17. Builds on [ADR-0008](0008-plan-ad-volume-counts-every-launched-ad-and-reads-a-hit-rate.md).

Once the plan divides by the Hit rate, a low one turns into a launch ask no team can make: a dry run asked an account with a single-figure hit rate to roughly double its weekly launches. Doubling output is not the lever there; raising the share of ads that work is. The skill now names a Focus from two readings, the Hit rate (Low under 8%, Fair 8% to under 15%, Strong 15% and over) and the ask (Launches a week to keep up over the Current pipeline): Hit rate first for a Low hit rate, or a Fair one with an ask above 1.5; Both for a Fair hit rate with an ask of 1.5 or less; Volume for a Strong hit rate. Hit rate first also moves If more of your ads work up to straight after the tiles. We considered capping the Week-by-week plan at 1.5 times the Current pipeline and rejected it: a capped plan stops closing the gap and hides what a low hit rate costs. Instead, where the ask is above 1.5, the hit-rate scenarios add a row giving the Hit rate needed to keep up at 1.5 times today's launches, the most production can stretch to. The thresholds are a judgement, not measured from accounts, and are worth revisiting once more runs exist.
