# audit-ads publishes the ad fatigue method in full, thresholds included

**Status:** Accepted, 2026-11-06.

`audit-ads` judges Meta ads with the fatigue model we run in client builds: CAC normalised by day of week, a 5-day window against the ad's best 7-day window in the last 42 days, Fatiguing at 20% and Fatigued at 50% worse than that peak with streak gates, Low efficiency at 20% above the Account blend, and the Eligibility floors (£300, 2 days, £30 a day). This repo is public, so writing the method into the skill publishes it to anyone. We publish it in full anyway, because the plugin ships craft and the brain ships mechanism (mcp_brain ADR-0026), and keeping the numbers in the brain would make the skill brain-only, which ADR-0004 rules out. What a brain still adds is the judging basis: one attribution source for every ad, not every purchase Meta claims. Once published, the method cannot be taken back, and a client build that later tunes these thresholds diverges from the public skill unless both change together.
