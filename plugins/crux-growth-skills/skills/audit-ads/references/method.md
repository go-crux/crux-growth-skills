Every Meta ad is judged on the last 60 complete days, one ad at a time. Here is how.

**1. One measure: CAC.** CAC is an ad's spend divided by the new customers it brought. With a Growth Brain these are counted from your own first orders, not from the purchases Meta claims; without one, they are the purchases Meta reports for the ad, which counts every purchase Meta claims. Each day's CAC is adjusted for the day of the week (some weekdays are always dearer) before any comparison, so a dear weekday is not mistaken for fatigue.

**2. Two windows.** The current window is the last 5 complete days. An ad's Peak CAC is its best (lowest) 7-day CAC on any of the last 42 days, as it stood that day. Fatigue is the current window against the Peak CAC.

**3. Not every ad is judged yet.** An ad needs all three of: 300 spent in its life, at least 2 days old, and 30 a day on average over the 5-day window (all in the account's currency). Below any of them it is Ramping: listed, with no verdict and no Recommendation.

**4. Account blend.** The blend is the CAC of all live ads together over the same 5 days. Ads in your retargeting and existing-customer campaigns are judged against the blend of those campaigns alone, and every other ad against the rest, because the two kinds of audience cost very different amounts.

**5. One status per ad, the first that fits:**

1. **Low efficiency**: 5-day CAC is 20% or more above its blend.
2. **Fatigued**: 5-day CAC is 50% or more above the Peak CAC on each of the last 3 days, and not improving.
3. **Fatiguing**: 20% or more above the Peak CAC on each of the last 2 days, and not improving.
4. **Improving**: 20% or more above the Peak CAC, but CAC has fallen over the last 3 days.
5. **Winner**: 5-day CAC is 20% or more below its blend.
6. **Healthy**: none of the above.

**6. What each status leads to.**

- **Pause**: every Low efficiency ad, and every Fatigued ad alongside its Refresh. A Low efficiency ad is paused however few ads its set holds, because it is losing money now. A Fatigued ad's Pause never leaves its ad set with fewer than 3 live ads past Ramping; where it would, it reads "Pause once the Refresh is live". A Pause that leaves its ad set with no ad past Ramping says so.
- **Refresh**: a new variation of a Fatiguing or Fatigued ad's concept.
- **Iterate**: more variations of a Winner's concept. Never a budget change.
- **Keep**: Healthy, Improving and Ramping ads, with no Recommendation.

**7. Size and confidence.** A Pause is sized as the extra CAC over the blend, times the new customers in the 5 days, run-rated to a month. A Refresh is sized the same way against the ad's own Peak CAC. An Iterate is sized as its CAC gap to the blend and its share of the last 7 days' spend. Confidence is High with 20 or more new customers in the 5 days, Medium with 8 to 19, Low with fewer.

**8. What the page shows.** Every ad is judged; only Reported ads (100 or more spent over the last 7 days) appear on it.
