# Opportunity catalogue

Each row is a pattern. The numbers and triggers are for the agent only: the page and the chat
name an opportunity by its own plain title, never by this number. Test its **trigger** against the figures the Read produced for last year.
"The year before" below means the year before last year. A pattern fires when its trigger holds on figures
that are not "No data". Percentages are relative.

| # | Pattern | Trigger in the read's figures | Action for this year | Metric to watch on the day |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Deep discount with low 90-day reorder | Black Friday weekend discount depth is deeper than Baseline's, and the 90-day reorder share of Black Friday weekend new customers is at least 20% below Baseline new customers' | Cap the deepest rung or move it to existing customers, and put a second-order offer in the weekend's welcome flow | Share of weekend new customers entering the welcome flow, and discount depth by hour |
| 2 | Lead-in conversion below Baseline | Lead-in conversion is more than 5% below Baseline conversion | Hold the main offer for the weekend and use the Lead-in for list-only early access, so shoppers who wait are not trained to wait | Daily conversion through the Lead-in against the Baseline figure |
| 3 | A hero product carrying most of the weekend's margin | One product holds 40% or more of Black Friday weekend contribution margin | Secure stock for it above the plan, and pair it with a second product in a bundle to spread the margin | Hero product's stock cover in days, and its share of the day's margin |
| 4 | A high-discount product at low margin | A top-five weekend product has average discount deeper than the weekend average and margin % less than half the weekend's | Cut its discount, lift its price or drop it from the offer | That product's margin % and units through the day |
| 5 | Acquisition CAC on the weekend above Baseline | Black Friday weekend CAC is more than 20% above Baseline CAC | Set a daily CAC ceiling before the weekend and move spend above it to the best landing pages or to retention | Blended CAC per day against the ceiling |
| 6 | A landing page converting well below the site | A landing page with 5% or more of weekend visits converts at less than half the site's weekend conversion | Send paid traffic to the page that converts best, or rebuild the weak page before the Lead-in | That page's conversion and its share of visits |
| 7 | A Klaviyo campaign outperforming the rest | In a window with three or more campaigns, one campaign's revenue is at least twice the average of the others | Rebuild this year's sends on that campaign's offer and timing, and resend it to non-openers | Revenue per send for the matching send this year |
| 8 | Repeat share falling over Festive | Returning customers' share of Festive orders is more than 10% below the same share in the year before | Plan a Festive retention flow for Black Friday weekend buyers, led by a second-purchase offer | Returning customers' share of daily orders through Festive |
| 9 | Email send cadence off the benchmark | Last year's campaigns on any day of Black Friday weekend fall below the send cadence benchmark, or the weekend's campaigns total more than twice the benchmark's 5 while revenue per send sits below Baseline's | Below: add sends to the light days, led by the weekend's best offer and a last-chance send on Cyber Monday. Above: cut the weakest sends and resend the best to non-openers | Campaigns sent and revenue per send, day by day against the benchmark |
| 10 | Black Friday customers worth less over time | Last year's Black Friday weekend cohort's 180-day margin per customer is at least 25% below its Baseline cohort's, or its one-order share at least 10% above | Point weekend acquisition at full-price and hero products rather than the deepest rung, and put a timed second-order offer in the welcome flow for weekend customers | Weekend new customers' discount depth, and their second-order rate at 30 days |

**Send cadence benchmark** (pattern 9): from operators' own Black Friday calendars, a brand
sends at least 2 email campaigns on Black Friday and 1 on each of Saturday, Sunday and Cyber
Monday, 5 over the weekend.

Where a margin comes from a Shopify Source rather than a Growth Brain, the margin that patterns
3, 4 and 10 read is the gross profit of `SKILL.md` step 1's Basis bullet ("Gross profit, as
Shopify records it", in place of the contribution margin they name; pattern 10's 180-day margin
per customer is cumulative gross profit per customer), and a margin that reads "No data" stops
them firing. Patterns 7 and 9 read no margin; they cannot fire where Retention reads "No
data".

Add a row when a season shows a pattern the catalogue missed. A new row carries all three
columns and a trigger that a figure in the Read can settle.
