# Glossary

The terms `SKILL.md` and the other references use, each with one meaning.

- **Find**: the mode that ranks the brand's landing pages by Revenue at stake from the Growth Brain, so the user picks which to audit. Needs a brain. _Avoid_: discovery, scan.

- **Audit**: the mode that scores one landing page, given as a URL, against the six ingredients and turns the failed checks into findings and a test backlog, with the page's own performance beside it where a brain holds it. Runs without a brain. _Avoid_: critique, review, teardown.

- **Brand guardrails**: what the brand will not do or say (levers off the table, regulated claims) and who the page is for, inferred from the live site and confirmed in one short round of questions, then kept in `brand-guardrails.md` for later runs. A recommendation that breaches one is invalid. _Avoid_: brand constraints, brand rules.

- **Page type**: what a landing page is, read from its Shopify path: product (`/products/`), collection (`/collections/`), landing page (`/pages/`), blog (`/blogs/`) or home (`/`). Pages compare only within their type. _Avoid_: template, page category.

- **Benchmark**: what a page's visits would earn at the site's rate for the same Page type and channel, falling back to the Page type alone where a channel's cell is thin. From a Shopify export, which carries no channel, the Page type rate alone. _Avoid_: average, site average, baseline.

- **Revenue at stake**: a page's visits times the gap between its Benchmark revenue per visit and its own, from a Growth Brain. What Find ranks on. _Avoid_: lost revenue, opportunity, potential.

- **Orders at stake**: the same gap in conversion rather than revenue, from a Shopify export where no brain is connected. _Avoid_: lost orders.

- **Underperforming page**: a page with at least 1% of the site's visits in the period and at least 20% below its Benchmark. Checkout, cart, account and Unknown pages are never ranked. _Avoid_: weak page, problem page.
