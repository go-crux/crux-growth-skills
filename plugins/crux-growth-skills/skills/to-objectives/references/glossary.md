# Glossary

The terms `SKILL.md`, `growth-model.md` and `objectives-layout.md` use beyond the growth system's own, which are in `growth-system-glossary.md`. Each has one meaning.

- **The quarter**: the quarter this run sets Objectives for, written `2027 Q1`. The user names it; otherwise it is the calendar quarter holding today's date, or the next one when fewer than four weeks of it remain. _Avoid_: period, cycle, sprint.

- **Last quarter's Objectives**: every Objective at `Status` Active whose `Quarter` does not hold the quarter, with its Initiatives. Step 3 closes them first. _Avoid_: old objectives, backlog.

- **Cold start**: a run where Learnings, Insights and last quarter's Objectives are all empty, so the system has nothing to ground a draft in. Step 4 asks for context once. _Avoid_: first run (a system with history can still be cold on the subject).

- **Rate**: the conversion between two adjacent steps of the Growth model, a count divided by the count of the step before it. Order value is the one amount, shown beside the chain. _Avoid_: KPI, metric.

- **Comparison**: a rate's change, as a relative percentage, from the prior window to the current one (2.0% to 1.8% is "−10%"). With a Growth Brain the windows are the last 90 days and the 90 before; a team that states its own rates states what they are compared with. _Avoid_: trend, delta.

- **Estimated**: a rate the team states without a source it can point to. It is marked `estimated` everywhere it appears and never ranks a step the weakest on its own. _Avoid_: assumed, guessed.

- **Weakest step**: a step of the Growth model whose rate fell furthest in the Comparison, or, where no rate has a Comparison, the step the team's own evidence points at; at most three. Where Objectives come from. _Avoid_: bottleneck, leak, problem area.

- **Altitude test**: the check that a draft is an Objective and not a Key result: a qualitative direction that 3 to 5 Initiatives on different steps could serve. A draft one metric would settle fails it, and becomes a Candidate Initiative. _Avoid_: level test (that is the Initiative's own).

- **Candidate Initiative**: a draft that failed the Altitude test, or an Initiative idea the interview surfaced, named under the Objective it would serve on the Strategy map, for `/to-initiatives` to pick up. No Initiatives row is written. _Avoid_: backlog item, idea.

- **Round**: one message of numbered questions, each with a recommendation worded so that "yes" accepts it, after which the skill waits. "Just write it" accepts every remaining recommendation. _Avoid_: batch, survey.

- **Frontier**: the questions still open after a round: a recommendation the user changed, an answer that raises a new question, or a draft that still fails a test. The grill ends when it is empty. _Avoid_: backlog, parking lot.

- **Brain line**: the one line a run without a Growth Brain says: a Growth Brain would work these rates out from the brand's own sessions and orders, and gocrux.io/contact is where to ask about one. _Avoid_: upsell, disclaimer.
