# Glossary

The terms `SKILL.md` and `tasks-layout.md` use beyond the growth system's own, which are in `growth-system-glossary.md`. Each has one meaning.

- **Cut**: the whole set of Tasks and Experiments drafted from one Project, new and updated alike, with their dates. Proposed as one table, agreed on one yes, written together. _Avoid_: breakdown, backlog, plan.

- **Drafted Task**: a Task or Experiment of the Cut that is not written yet: its sections, `Runs as`, `Due` and what blocks it. _Avoid_: candidate, proposed card.

- **Matched Task**: a non-archived Task already under the Project that does the same work as a Drafted Task, matched on the work and not the wording. At `To review` or `Done` it is left untouched; otherwise it is updated in place, keeping its `ID`. _Avoid_: duplicate, existing card.

- **Unmatched Task**: a non-archived Task already under the Project that matches no Drafted Task. Reported and left as it is: archiving it is a person's call. _Avoid_: orphan, stale task.

- **Gap**: a fact the Cut needs and the Project does not carry (a parameter, an Open question with no owner or date, a `Due` that is blank or lapsed). Reported for `/to-projects`, and the Task that needs it is marked blocked on it; never filled with the nearest plausible value. _Avoid_: TBD, assumption.

- **Blocker**: one line under a Task's `Blocked by`: an Open question, by its wording on the Project, or another Task under the same Project, by its title while drafting and by its `ID` once created. _Avoid_: dependency (that is the relation between two Tasks, a Blocker is the line that records it), prerequisite.

- **Run order**: the order the work runs in, which is the order of the proposal table and of the `## Tasks` checklist. A Task comes after every Task named in its `Blocked by`. It blocks nothing by itself: only a written Blocker does. _Avoid_: priority, rank.

- **Verdict date**: an Experiment's `Due`: the date its result is read, when its `Done when` can be checked. It may fall after the Project's `Due`. _Avoid_: end date, launch date.

- **Sitting**: the one session in which an approver reviews the week's sign-offs, so sign-offs that share an approver and a week share a date. A Sitting that collects three or more sign-offs is named as a risk. _Avoid_: batch, review slot.

- **Question**: one thing asked of the user while scoping an Experiment (the variants, the primary KPI, the baseline), alone in its message, with a recommendation and its reason. "Yes" accepts the recommendation and "just write it" accepts every remaining one. _Avoid_: round.
