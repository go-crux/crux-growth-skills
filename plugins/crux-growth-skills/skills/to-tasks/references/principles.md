# The four principles

The growth system runs on four principles from Brian Balfour. Each is backed by a house rule the skills enforce, and every growth-system skill cites the principle a step serves. The same four are published, in Crux voice, at https://gocrux.io/growth-system.

## Maximise learning

AI can run more tests than any team could before, and each one is only worth running if what it taught you is documented and used as context for your next agent session.

**House rule:** a result becomes a Learning on the evidence ladder (Proven, Validated, Directional, Anecdotal), and the context load reads Active Learnings before every plan.

## Seek impact

With AI, ideas are cheap and abundant, so picking the right one to focus on is a lever for growth in itself. Prioritising from a strong data foundation and customer context gives you a higher hit rate.

**House rule:** ICE ranks Experiments, Impact × Urgency ranks Issues, and Projects are chosen on `Priority` against their Initiative. An Active Decision is flagged when a proposal would breach it, so effort goes where the team has already said it should.

## Balance art and science

With access to more data than ever, managing context becomes a key part of the growth team's job, so that AI output is high quality.

**House rule:** every learning states its evidence strength and its sources, and Insights keep the customer's own words beside the numbers. The context load names what it read, so the user can judge the context behind a proposal.

## Stay agile

When a test can be built in an afternoon, the plan has to change as fast as the evidence does.

**House rule:** a Decision is never edited into another: a replacement supersedes it, and a breach is flagged rather than worked around, so the plan can change in the open. WIP limits on the Tasks board keep work moving.
